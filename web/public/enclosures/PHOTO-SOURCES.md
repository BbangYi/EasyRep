# EasyRep reference photographs

Verified from Wikimedia Commons file-description pages on 2026-09-16. Downloaded photographs have not been edited. These are illustrative species photographs, not photographs of the demo's own enclosures or proof of live monitoring.

## Recommended three

### 1. crested-gecko.jpg
- Species: crested gecko, Correlophus ciliatus.
- Content: real animal on green foliage. A planted-vivarium setting is not established by the source, so do not describe it as a verified enclosure photograph.
- Author: Lennart Hudel (lennarthud on iNaturalist).
- License: CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Crested_gecko_-_1.jpg
- Original source: https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/33606417
- Download URL: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d9/Crested_gecko_-_1.jpg
- Downloaded size: 1280 × 853.
- 16:9 crop: use the full width; object-position 50% 55%. Preserve the eye near the lower left and the green leaves. A 1280 × 720 crop loses some upper foliage and part of the lower toes but keeps the head and body.
- Suggested credit: 크레스티드 게코 사진: Lennart Hudel / Wikimedia Commons / CC BY 4.0. 카드 비율에 맞춰 크롭.

### 2. leopard-gecko-habitat.jpg
- Species: leopard gecko, Eublepharis macularius.
- Content: animal resting on a ceramic hide, with rocks and enclosure floor visible. This is the strongest habitat-context image of the set.
- Author: Asklēpiós.
- License: CC BY-SA 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Leopard_gecko_basking.jpg
- Download URL: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/3a/Leopard_gecko_basking.jpg/1280px-Leopard_gecko_basking.jpg
- Downloaded size: 1280 × 720, already 16:9.
- Crop: use as-is, object-position 50% 50%. The animal sits near the top; avoid further vertical cropping or top overlays.
- Suggested credit: 레오파드 게코 사진: Asklēpiós / Wikimedia Commons / CC BY-SA 4.0. 축소 사용.
- If an adapted image is distributed, preserve the same or compatible share-alike license for that image adaptation.

- Species: ball python, Python regius.
- Content: real pet ball python beneath a rock-shaped hide with loose substrate visible; animal-focused enclosure photograph.

## Reuse implementation

Keep credits in a visible photo-credit section or linked attribution panel. For CC BY and CC BY-SA photographs, link both source page and license, name the author, and state cropping/resizing when applied. The photos are illustration/reference material and do not establish enclosure settings or husbandry recommendations.
